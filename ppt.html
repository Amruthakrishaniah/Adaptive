The Problem with Conventional Side Stands

Standard motorcycle stands use a small metal foot that can slip on wet surfaces and sink into mud or sand. This causes the motorcycle to tilt and potentially fall.

🌧️

Slips on Wet Roads

Low-friction metal feet lose traction on rain-soaked pavement and tiles.

🏖️

Sinks into Soft Ground

Narrow contact areas concentrate load into mud, sand, and loose gravel.

⚠️

Risk of Falling

Unstable support can cause expensive vehicle damage and safety hazards.

How the Product Works

Three biological inspirations combine to create a highly stable parking system.

🦶 Human Foot-Inspired Base

The broad sole-shaped base spreads the motorcycle's weight over a larger area, reducing pressure and preventing sinking into soft terrain.

🦎 Gecko-Inspired Grip

A micro-textured TPU pad increases surface friction, improving traction on wet, smooth, and slippery ground.

💪 Shoulder Joint Mechanism

The rotational pivot allows the base to align naturally with uneven surfaces for full ground contact.

🔒 Passive Auto-Locking

Once the motorcycle's weight is applied, the stand locks securely in place without requiring electronics.
